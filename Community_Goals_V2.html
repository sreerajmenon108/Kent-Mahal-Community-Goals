Financial Empowerment

Community Chitty System (KSFE Model)

Implementing a trusted peer-to-peer chitty system modeled on KSFE and local chitty companies. Members contribute a fixed monthly sum, creating a secure internal financial pool for member liquidity and community surplus.

How It Works

Members pay monthly installments. Each month, an auction or draw allocates the pool to a needy member at a discount.

Community Benefits

Generates dividend bonuses distributed to all participants while funding community welfare projects.

Implementation Highlights Trusted & Secure
1
Transparent member registration & clear agreement guidelines.
2
Monthly meetings or digital tracking managed via office transparency.
3
Dividends shared among all non-prized subscribers every cycle.
