05

Community Chitty System

KSFE-inspired peer financial rotation for community growth

How It Typically Works

Members contribute a fixed monthly installment into a pooled community fund. Through a transparent monthly auction or draw, a subscriber receives the pooled amount for personal needs or emergency liquidity.

Community Benefits

Provides trusted peer-to-peer financial assistance without external banking hurdles. A portion of auction dividends or management contributions directly builds association welfare and reserve funds.
